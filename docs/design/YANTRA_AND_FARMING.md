## 1. Terrace Agricultural Architecture

Settling the slopes of the Kathmandu Valley requires mastering the landscape through terracing. Unaltered hillsides erode quickly under torrential monsoon rainfall; stepped terraces transform rugged gradients into productive, flat micro-fields.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| KHET (Lowland Irrigated Terraces)             BARI (Upland Rain-Fed Terraces)     |
| - Level bunds retaining standing water.       - Sloping outward to shed excess    |
```

## 2. Waterways & Irrigation Channels (*Kulo*)

Agriculture in **Dewabhumi** relies on gravity-fed irrigation channels (*Kulo*) excavated from natural mountain streams:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    River[Mountain Stream] -->|Diversion Weir| Kulo[Main Irrigation Canal]
    Kulo -->|Field Sluice| Terrace1[Upper Terrace]
    Terrace1 -->|Spillway| Terrace2[Lower Terrace]
    Terrace2 -->|Drainage| Tailwater[Valley Drainage Basin]
```

### Flow Dynamics
- **Diversion Weirs (*Bandh*):** Constructed from timber pilings, woven brushwood, and river boulders to deflect water into canals without obstructing natural river volume.
- **Canal Maintenance:** Channels accumulate silt during heavy rains. Villagers must clear debris annually before sowing, or water flow drops by 50%.
- **Gravity Rule:** Canals can only flow downhill. Tracing a canal across contour lines requires careful leveling.

## 3. The Traditional Himalayan Crop Cycle

The agricultural calendar revolves around the monsoon rhythm:

| Season | Months | Primary Agricultural Activities | Main Harvest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grishma (Pre-Monsoon Dry)** | Chaitra – Jestha | Plowing dry fields with draft oxen, spreading compost/manure, preparing paddy nurseries. | Early barley, wild herbs. |
| **Varsha (Monsoon Deluge)** | Asar – Shrawan | Transplanting wet paddy rice seedlings (*Ropain* festival), weeding upland millet. | Abundant fresh grass & milk. |
| **Sharad (Autumn Clear)** | Bhadra – Ashwin | Draining paddy fields, drying grain on communal courtyards, threshing and winnowing. | **Main Harvest:** Rice (*Dhan*), Millet (*Kodo*). |
| **Hemanta / Shishir (Winter)** | Kartik – Magh | Sowing hardy winter crops (barley, mustard, lentils), scything dry terraces. | Mustard oilseeds, winter vegetables. |

## 4. Yantra: Traditional Hydraulic Machinery

As settlements advance, players unlock traditional water-powered mechanisms (*Yantra*):

### The Traditional Watermill (*Pani Ghatta*)
- **Function:** Harnesses stream velocity via a wooden turbine wheel to turn heavy granite grinding stones.
- **Productivity:** Mills grain into fine flour 10x faster than manual hand-querns (*Janto*), freeing dozens of villager labor hours for farming and construction.
